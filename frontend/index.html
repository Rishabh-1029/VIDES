IN SHORT

Detection asks “what is in this frame?” Tracking asks “is this the same vehicle as before?” The event checks use those answers across time.

A / PERSPECTIVE

Flatten the road

p′ ∼ H · p

A homography matrix H maps a point p from the camera image to point p′ on a flattened road plane. The four zone corners define this mapping.

What does this mean?

Nearby road edges look larger than distant ones in a camera image. Mapping the selected quadrilateral to a rectangle makes movement easier to measure consistently.

B / SCALE

Convert pixels to metres

S = L / Dpx

L is the known road length represented by the zone along the arrow direction. Dpx is that span on the flattened plane. S is metres per pixel.

Where does the road length fit?

Measure the road length represented by the full zone in the direction of traffic and enter it in Configure. VIDES estimates the matching pixel span from the flattened view and flow angle.

C / SPEED

Estimate vehicle speed

vkm/h = (Δp × S / Δt) × 3.6

Δp is movement in projected pixels, S converts pixels to metres, and Δt is elapsed time. The factor 3.6 converts metres per second to km/h.

Why is it an estimate?

Speed quality depends on the zone corners, measured distance, frame rate, perspective, and tracking stability. VIDES is a camera-based estimate, not a calibrated road sensor.

FLOW VECTORf(θ) = (sin θ, −cos θ)

VIDES uses this direction for comparison: 0° points up, 90° right, 180° down, and 270° left.

HIGH

Overspeed

Estimated speed rises above the configured speed limit. The event includes both the measured speed and limit; each vehicle track is recorded once.

speed > speed limit
HIGH

Wrong direction

After enough tracked positions, movement is strongly opposite to the expected flow arrow. Each track is recorded once.

cos(angle) < −0.75
HIGH

Stalled vehicle

A tracked vehicle remains within about one metre for at least five seconds inside the selected area.

distance < 1 m · time ≥ 5 s
CRITICAL

Possible collision

Two vehicles already marked as stalled have strongly overlapping detection boxes. It is a prompt for review, not confirmation of a crash.

box overlap (IoU) > 0.5
MEDIUM

Heavy vehicle

A detected truck has a calibrated bounding-box diagonal greater than six metres.

estimated diagonal > 6 m
EVENT RECORD

Each record contains a unique ID, event type, vehicle track ID(s), severity, timestamp, and an explanation. Collision records include both vehicle IDs.

TOOLS & MODEL CHOICES

Learn the parts behind detection

The active dashboard pipeline and the companion research models have different jobs.

CV / 02
YACTIVE DETECTOR

YOLOv8

Looks at one frame and predicts vehicle locations, classes, and confidence. The default VIDES weights are Custom_yolov8m.pt.

Why confidence matters

A lower threshold accepts weaker predictions and may find more vehicles with more false alarms. A higher threshold is more selective and may miss partly hidden vehicles.

TIDENTITY OVER TIME

DeepSORT

Matches new boxes to recent tracks so a vehicle can keep the same ID from frame to frame.

How matching works

DeepSORT combines motion prediction and appearance features. VIDES uses its MobileNet embedder when available and has an HSV feature fallback.

CVVIDEO & GEOMETRY

OpenCV + BEV

OpenCV reads and draws video frames. A perspective transform turns the selected road shape into a bird’s-eye view for measuring movement.

What is a bird’s-eye view?

It is a top-down representation of the road area. It reduces perspective distortion for the system’s movement and speed calculations.

MODEL WORKBENCH

Custom models, with context

VIDES includes a selectable YOLOv8 detector. These related projects show other ways to approach vehicle vision.

LEARN / 03
USED IN THIS DASHBOARDACTIVE

VIDES custom YOLOv8

A one-stage detector predicts boxes and labels directly from an image. It is the live model family in the current application.

Model selection in VIDES

The default is models/Custom_yolov8m.pt. Configure can load another included .pt file. VIDES releases the old model and waits for the new one to finish loading; only one is active at a time.

Faster R-CNN

A two-stage detector first proposes candidate regions, then classifies and refines them. The custom vehicle model is trained and evaluated separately from the live YOLOv8 pipeline.
